P|Ques 3 ->|What is Medication history Interview?

P|Ans ->|A Structured interview Conducted by a Pharmacist to gather a complete record of a Patient's current prescription, OTC (over the Counter), and herbal medication use to prevent reconciliation errors.

P|Ques 4 ->|Define Adverse Drug Reaction (ADR).

P|Ans ->|Any noxious unintended, and undesired response to a drug that occurs at doses normally used in human for prophylaxis diagnosis, or therapy.
